How long has it been since I published something... 10 MONTHS? oops. (knife sharpening 
notes notwithstanding) Frankly, I have no idea how many people read this blog. I don't
have analytics, at all. Not even a count of page loads. I don't know if I was missed,
but I like to think I was. Life has been a lot, in a good way. I finished my thesis,
and a BA in computer science. It was an expository thesis on cryptography, and frankly 
any more specificity
than that will make my real name searchable. Writing a thesis is truly an all-consuming
endeavor. It takes all your available time and then grasps for more. It's an academic 
pressure cooker like no other. Thanks to the hard work of my forebears, an excellent
advisor, and my own hard work, I made it. I'm happy with the work I did, and it needed to 
be done, but I plan on doing more in that vein in my personal capacity.

Going to college has been the hardest and best thing in my life so far. I'm glad that I
did a Bachelor of Arts in computer science (as opposed to a Bachelor of Science), because
holy *fuck* do people in CS need to touch grass. I consider it my job to increase the
average amount of grass touching in the field. I have more wonderful friends than high
school me would have thought possible. I got well-paying job doing technical writing 
right out of school, which I think is equal parts luck and the amount of work I've put
into having a kick-ass resume. Doing so much technical writing for work *has* made me
write a little less for fun, mostly because I want to spend less time at the computer.

Basically, I'm doing great overall. The transition from school into the workforce has 
been wacky. It's going surprisingly smoothly considering this fuck ass job market. 
I'm loving life and trying to improve the world, while not putting too much pressure on
myself. I'm capable of great things, but great man theory is not real and all. Peace 
and love on planet earth. Expect more writing soon, and hopefully a blog redesign.
